HIGH-FIDELITY MODELING PLAN: BIOLOGICAL ENTRAINMENT CHAIN

Goal: Transition from random stress spikes to a network of coupled biological oscillators to find genuine synchrony signal.

1. CIRCADIAN ALIGNMENT 
- Reference: phillips_2017_sleep_circadian.pdf
- Logic: Replace static days with a 24h master clock for each individual.
- Mechanism: Model roommates with "shared social constraints" (aligned sleep/wake and light exposure). This aligns the household to a shared circadian phase.

2. DIURNAL HPA DYNAMICS
- Reference: zavala_2020_stress_fertility.pdf
- Logic: Replace "stress spikes" with a continuous HPA axis model (CRH-ACTH-Cortisol).
- Mechanism: Cortisol production is gated by the circadian phase, creating a shared diurnal background. Stressors amplify this existing rhythm.

3. SOCIAL CONTAGION
- Reference: palumbo_2017_physio_sync.pdf
- Logic: Implement weak Kuramoto-like coupling between the HPA oscillators of roommates.
- Mechanism: Represents "Interpersonal Physiological Synchrony": emotional contagion and shared domestic environment. Stress in one individual slightly elevates the baseline stress of others.

4. MECHANISTIC GnRH INHIBITION
- Reference: voliotis_2019_gnrh_generator.pdf
- Logic: Use the KNDy neuronal model for GnRH pulse generation.
- Mechanism: The oscillating shared Cortisol background directly modulates the frequency of the pulse generator.

5. MENSTRUAL HPG INTEGRATION
- Reference: fischer_2022_menstrual_model.pdf (HormonalRegOvaFolCycInHuman.pdf)
- Logic: Integrate the GnRH inhibition into the 54-equation mechanistic menstrual model.
- Goal: Continuous "massaging" force on the follicular phase provides a much stronger candidate for dynamical entrainment.
